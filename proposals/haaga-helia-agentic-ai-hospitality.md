## Core positioning

Agentic AI is the next level in the practical evolution of AI: from isolated model outputs to AI systems that can understand context, use tools, work with business data, follow workflows, ask for approval and produce traceable results.

For hospitality this means a new operational capability:

- guest-facing agents for communication, feedback and service recovery;
- commercial agents for sales, marketing, segmentation, pricing and revenue support;
- operations agents for SOPs, shift handovers, procurement and internal coordination;
- back-office agents for documents, invoices, reporting and compliance;
- governance patterns that keep human judgment, brand standards and data protection in control.

## Signature framework: Hospitality Agent Map

The course is built around five hospitality agent domains.

### 1. Guest Experience Agents

Use cases:

- multilingual guest messaging;
- review and feedback analysis;
- complaint triage and service recovery;
- pre-arrival and post-stay communication;
- loyalty and personalization support.

Core idea:

The agent does not replace hospitality. It protects consistency, speed and memory, while staff keep empathy and final judgment.

### 2. Commercial Agents

Use cases:

- segment discovery from booking and CRM data;
- campaign planning for specific guest segments;
- corporate and group sales support;
- upsell and cross-sell recommendations;
- competitor and market signal monitoring.

Core idea:

The agent connects customer intelligence with concrete commercial actions.

### 3. Revenue and Distribution Agents

Use cases:

- demand signal monitoring;
- pricing memo preparation;
- channel mix and margin analysis;
- occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and TRevPAR support;
- event-calendar and competitor-watch workflows.

Core idea:

The agent prepares evidence and options. The revenue manager makes the decision.

### 4. Operations and F&B Agents

Use cases:

- shift handover summaries;
- SOP assistant for staff;
- procurement and supplier communication;
- menu engineering and food-waste notes;
- maintenance and incident follow-up.

Core idea:

The agent reduces operational friction and helps knowledge move across shifts, teams and locations.

### 5. Back-Office and Governance Agents

Use cases:

- invoice and document processing;
- contract and policy search;
- management reporting;
- internal knowledge base;
- audit trail, permissions and approval workflows.

Core idea:

The agent makes routine administrative work more structured, but sensitive actions stay controlled and traceable.

## Demo lesson

**From Guest Signals to Service Recovery Actions**

Flow:

1. Import guest reviews, emails and internal notes.
2. Agent classifies issues by topic, severity and business impact.
3. Agent drafts response options in brand tone.
4. Agent escalates high-risk cases to a manager.
5. Agent creates weekly insights for operations, marketing and revenue.
6. Team reviews the actions and updates SOPs.

Why this demo works:

- it is immediately hospitality-specific;
- it shows agentic AI as a workflow, not a writing tool;
- it connects guest experience, operations and management;
- it is understandable for non-technical managers;
- it opens a path to more advanced agents in revenue, marketing and back office.
